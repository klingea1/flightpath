# Sounds

The hatch and the card scenes play these files. Each must be CC0 (public
domain), MP3, and small: aim for under 1.5 MB for all six together. A missing
file is simply silent.

| File | Used for | Length | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `crack.mp3` | the egg cracking | under 1 s | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
| `flutter.mp3` | a burst of wings at the hatch | 1–2 s | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
| `wind.mp3` | the default ambience, looped | 10–30 s loop | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
| `chorus.mp3` | dawn scenes, looped | 10–30 s loop | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
| `owl.mp3` | the night scene, played once | 2–4 s | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
| `gulls.mp3` | the Lakeshore Pilgrim persona, looped | 5–15 s | (owner to fill in) | CC0 |
