# Sounds

The hatch and the card scenes play these files. Each must be CC0 (public
domain), MP3, and small: aim for under 1.5 MB for all six together. A missing
file is simply silent.

| File | Used for | Length | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `crack.mp3` | the egg cracking (each peck and the seam) | 0.5 s | ["egg crack19.wav" by Reitanna](https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/244745/) | CC0 |
| `flutter.mp3` | a burst of wings at the hatch and the Summary's thud | 1.5 s (2.5–4.0 s of the original) | ["FlapWings.aif" by framixo](https://freesound.org/people/framixo/sounds/463381/) | CC0 |
| `wind.mp3` | the default ambience, looped | 10 s loop, 4 dB quieter | ["26.07.17- Wind in Tree Leaves" by Valerie-Vivegnis](https://freesound.org/people/Valerie-Vivegnis/sounds/865353/) | CC0 |
| `chorus.mp3` | dawn scenes, looped | 15 s loop (the first 15 s) | ["Flock_of_Birds.m4a" by Blizzard123](https://freesound.org/people/Blizzard123/sounds/504750/) | CC0 |
| `owl.mp3` | evening and night scenes, played once | 4 s (2.5–6.5 s of the original) | ["Owl in Arkansas Backyard 01" by gfrog](https://freesound.org/people/gfrog/sounds/157870/) | CC0 |
| `gulls.mp3` | the Lakeshore Pilgrim persona, looped | 13.5 s loop | ["Herring Gull passing over roof" by genghisattenborough](https://freesound.org/people/genghisattenborough/sounds/744592/) | CC0 |

All six were converted with ffmpeg to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 96 kbps and
levelled to about -18 LUFS (`loudnorm`). Each has a few ms of fade at either
end so it doesn't click; the trims are noted above. About 540 KB in all.
